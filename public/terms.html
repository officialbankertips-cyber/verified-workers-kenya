Platform role

Verified Workers provides online tools for workers, employers and recruitment agencies to create profiles, publish vacancies and communicate about employment opportunities. The platform does not guarantee a job, candidate suitability or hiring outcome.

Registration fee

The registration fee is Ksh 199 as a one-time platform registration charge. A successful payment confirms payment only; it does not certify a person's identity, qualifications, licence, employment history or trustworthiness.

Accurate information

Users must provide accurate information and may not impersonate others, upload unlawful material, misrepresent qualifications or misuse personal information.

Verification

Verification badges are issued only after the platform's stated review process. Verification is limited to the documents and checks actually completed and is not a guarantee of future conduct or job performance.

Employers and agencies

Employers and agencies are responsible for lawful recruitment, fair hiring practices, appropriate candidate checks and compliance with applicable licensing and employment rules.

Suspension and contact

We may restrict accounts for suspected fraud, abuse or breach of these terms. Publish a monitored support email and phone number here before launch.
